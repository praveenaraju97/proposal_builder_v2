Introduction

 

 

PA 9B.00014 is a Residential Development consisting of two towers of (B+G+P+18)

with a common podium and amenities. The plot overlooks the Dubai Hills Golf Course,

and the surrounding low rise residential areas. The objective of the design is to create

high quality living environments that cater to contemporary, socially driven urban

lifestyles.

A focus on wellness and high standards of livability will set the design principles

of the development. The residential spaces will be designed to cater to the comfort and

wellbeing of its residents. This requires a focus on access to daylight, biophilic elements

and efficiency of internal layouts while maximizing views towards Dubai Hills Golf Course.

The common areas and facilities will be focal points of the development and provide high

quality social environments for its residents. Access to the recreational corridor and

park should be well studied.

 

The reference Project for both towers is Palace Residences in DHE. Total number of

Units is approximately 555 units.
